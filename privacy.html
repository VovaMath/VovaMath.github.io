Privacy and data use

Google permission: gmail.metadata. This allows access to message metadata, including labels and headers across the connected mailbox. It does not permit reading message bodies, sending mail, or modifying messages. The utility reads the From header to select Wyzant senders. Other senders are not passed to Codex.

The utility stores the configured account address, Gmail history cursor, Wyzant message identifiers, dispatch identifiers, receipts, and operational status locally. It does not store message bodies. Its event ledger remains on the owner's computer until the owner removes it.

OAuth credentials are encrypted on disk using Windows CurrentUser DPAPI. They are used to communicate with Google's Gmail API. The owner may revoke the utility's access in Google Account connections and stop the Windows task.

For matching Wyzant messages, Gmail identifiers and an event signal are sent to the owner's Codex chat through the installed Codex command. The chat may then read the relevant emails through the separately authorized Gmail connector and act on Wyzant according to the owner's instructions. Those providers' applicable terms and the owner's account settings govern their processing. The local utility does not itself operate an AI training, advertising, or data-sale service.

Use of information received through Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
